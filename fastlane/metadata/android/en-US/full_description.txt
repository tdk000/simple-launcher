Simple Launcher is a tiny home screen replacement: your apps in one
alphabetical grid over your own wallpaper, plus a clock and a search field
that searches the phone.

There are no pages, no dock, no widgets, no folders, no gestures, no icon
packs, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app declares zero
permissions and never touches the network.

Features:

* Grid of every launchable app, sorted A to Z by default.
* Alphabet index bar on the right (digits, Latin A-Z, Cyrillic A-Z, Hebrew):
  tap or drag to jump. Labels thin out when there are many sections, but touch
  still reaches all of them.
* Live search over installed apps by name or package name, any language,
  case-insensitive. The keyboard's search key opens the top local hit, or
  falls back to a web search when nothing on the phone matches.
* Clock and date over the wallpaper; translucent system bars.
* Long-press an app for app info or uninstall.
* Long-press the clock for sort order (six modes), hiding the search field,
  reloading the list, and switching the default launcher.
* Work profile support: badged icons, per-profile launch and app info, quiet
  profiles skipped.
* The list refreshes itself when packages are installed or removed.

The app runs on Android 5.0 (API 21) and up.
